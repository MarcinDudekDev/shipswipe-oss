# Builder ↔ Marketer Match App — Name & Market Research

## Market snapshot

**Problem:** Solo indie builders ship products but stall on distribution; growth marketers have channel expertise but lack products to promote. Both sides waste months on Twitter/Reddit/Discord hoping for a compatible partner.

**Competitors (brief):**

| Player | Focus | Gap vs. this product |
|---|---|---|
| [CoffeeSpace](https://www.coffeespace.com) | Hinge-style cofounder matching (30k+ users, 2.5M+ swipes) | Broad cofounder/hire — not builder↔marketer specific |
| [IndieMerger](https://indiemerger.com) | AI-matched indie builder directory (~1.8k verified) | Directory + AI scores, not swipe-first; marketers are a side use case |
| [YC Co-founder Matching](https://www.ycombinator.com/cofounder-matching) | YC-network cofounder intros | Gated, general cofounder — not growth-partner dyad |
| [OnlyFounders](https://onlyfounders.app) | "Tinder for founders" (7k+ users) | Founder↔founder; no marketer lane |
| [CoFoundersLab](https://cofounderslab.com) | Legacy cofounder marketplace | Listings/forums, dated UX, not swipe-native |

**Whitespace:** No dominant swipe-native product owns the **builder + marketer** pairing. Demand shows up organically (e.g. manual dev↔marketer matchmaking threads on Reddit). Position as the fast, fun way to find your *growth partner* — not another generic cofounder board.

## 1. Name candidates

| # | Name | Rationale | Domain (rough) |

| 3 | **LaunchMate** | Partnership for people trying to get something to market; warm, approachable. | `.io` likely available; `.com` taken |
| 4 | **ShipPair** | Builder who ships + the one person they need paired with. Short, literal. | `.io` likely available; `.com` taken |
| 5 | **MatchPitch** | Match + pitch/marketing angle; works if brand leans marketer-friendly. | `.io` likely available; `.com` taken |

*Domain checks via WHOIS, Jun 2026 — treat as indicative, not a purchase guarantee.*

---

## 2. Recommended pick: **ShipSwipe**

**Why:** Strongest recall and differentiation. Competitors sound like "cofounder" or "indie merger" tools; ShipSwipe instantly signals swipe UX *and* speaks the builder's language ("just ship it"). Available `.io` is on-brand for an indie SaaS product; `.com` being taken is acceptable at MVP stage. GrowPair is a solid #2 if you want a more neutral/professional tone, but ShipSwipe better supports a playful, demo-friendly brand.
