About ShipSwipe

We believe the hardest part of launching is not building the product — it is finding the person who can grow it.

Our mission

ShipSwipe exists to close the builder–marketer gap. Too many indie founders ship great software with no distribution plan. Too many growth people have audience and expertise but nothing compelling to promote. We make it easy to find the other half of a launch partnership.

What we are (and are not)

ShipSwipe is a focused matching tool — not a social network, not a job board, and not a fundraising platform. Profiles stay local to your browser. Swipes are intentional. Matches mean mutual interest. Connect flows help you start a real conversation about terms, timelines, and launch goals.

Built for indie operators

  • Rich profiles surface what someone builds, what they want, and how they want to partner.
  • Filters help you narrow by tags and deal terms before you swipe.
  • Undo, detail views, and intro messages respect that partnerships deserve a second look.

Why I built ShipSwipe

I shipped two indie SaaS products that stalled at launch because I could build but could not distribute. Every networking site felt like job hunting, not partnership building. ShipSwipe is the focused matcher I wished existed — pairing builders who need growth with marketers who need something real to promote.

— Alex Morgan, founder
