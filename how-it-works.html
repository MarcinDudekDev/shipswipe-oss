How ShipSwipe works

A simple flow designed for indie builders and growth marketers who need each other — not another generic networking site.

  1. Set up your profile

    Choose whether you are a builder or a marketer. Add a bio, what you are building or the channels you run, what partner you want, and your preferred deal terms — rev-share, equity, paid, or flexible.

  2. Discover and swipe

    You only see profiles on the opposite side. Filter by tags or terms, open full details before you decide, and pass or like each card. Made a mistake? Undo your last swipe.

  3. Match when interest is mutual

    If you like someone who already liked you, it is an instant match. Some seed profiles have already liked the demo user so you can experience a match right away.

  4. Connect and send an intro

    Matches appear on your matches page. Open the connect flow, see contact context, and send a short intro message to start the conversation about shipping and growing together.

After you match

Exchange contact details, agree on rev-share, equity, paid, or flexible terms, and start shipping together. ShipSwipe gets you to a mutual yes — the partnership work is yours.

Who ShipSwipe is for

ShipSwipe is not for cofounder hunting, hiring freelancers, or fundraising. It is for launch partnerships between builders who need distribution and marketers who need something real to grow.

Intro template

Send something concrete when you connect:

Hi [name] — loved your profile on [building/channels]. I am [your role] working on [your focus] and think we could launch together on [terms]. Open to a 20-min call this week?
